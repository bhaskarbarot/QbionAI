About us

A studio that ships,
not a lab that experiments

Qubion.Ai started in 2021 with three people and one belief: most companies do not need more AI strategy, they need one automated process that works by Friday.

Our story

Built out of frustration with slide decks

Our founders spent years inside agencies and product teams, watching AI projects die between the pilot and the P&L. The pattern was always the same: a strategy deck, a demo that impressed the board, and nothing anybody used six months later.

So we built the studio around the opposite constraint. Every engagement has to produce something running in production, owned by the client, documented well enough that we can walk away. If we cannot get there, we say so during the sprint and stop.

Today we are a team of 24 in Surat, working with companies across India, the UAE, the UK and Singapore.

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 August 2026. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you send a project brief we collect your name, email address, and anything else you choose to include — company, phone number, budget range and your description of the project. Our website also records standard analytics: pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referring source.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal
  • To deliver work you have contracted us for
  • To send our newsletter, but only if you asked for it
  • To understand which pages of this site are useful

What we never do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use client data to train models outside that client's own project. We do not share your brief with third parties beyond the processors listed below.

Processors we use

Email hosting, cloud storage, analytics and CRM are handled by established providers under data processing agreements. A current list is available on request at privacy@qubion.ai.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted after 24 months. Client records are retained for seven years where tax and contract law requires it. Newsletter data is removed as soon as you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@qubion.ai and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can block them in your browser without losing any functionality on this site.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to team members who need it for the project, reviewed quarterly, and revoked on the day someone leaves.
